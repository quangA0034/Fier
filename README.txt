FIER - a small farming game
===========================

HOW TO PLAY
  Open  index.html  in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
  No install, no server. Your progress is saved in the browser automatically.
  (The pixel font loads from Google Fonts. Offline it falls back to a plain font.)

CONTROLS
  WASD / Arrow keys   move
  E                   interact (plant, water, harvest, talk, enter buildings)
  1 2 3               pick seeds (carrot, wheat, potato)
  4 5                 drink a Beer / Gin from your bag
  Esc or P            pause
  M                   mute / unmute

GOAL
  Grow and sell crops, keep an eye on the mayor's mood, and save up 3000g
  for a one-way ticket to Germany.

FOLDERS
  fier-farm.html      the whole game (all art and sound is embedded in it)
  assets/sprites      crop, item, cat and penguin sprites (PNG, transparent)
  assets/audio        the processed sound files used by the game
  assets/map          your Tiled map files (fier.js / fier.tmj)
